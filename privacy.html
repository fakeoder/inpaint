Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

inpaint is a browser-based image editing tool operated by Zkraft. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the website.

Images and processing

Your selected images, masks, and generated results are processed in your browser. They are not uploaded to an inpaint server by the application. Large files can require significant device memory.

Model downloads and third-party services

On first use, an AI model may be downloaded from its configured hosting provider, which can receive a normal HTTP request including your IP address and standard browser information. The model is then cached by your browser. Review the relevant provider's privacy policy for its handling of that request.

Local storage

inpaint may use browser storage, including Cache Storage and IndexedDB, to cache AI models and save selected settings. This information stays on your device unless you clear it or your browser removes it.

Information you send us

If you email us, we receive the information you include in that message and use it to respond to your request. Contact: contact@zkraft.cc.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the product changes. Questions about privacy can be sent to contact@zkraft.cc.
